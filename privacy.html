Privacy Policy for BeamDocs

BeamDocs is provided by OfflineNode.

This Privacy Policy explains how BeamDocs handles your information.

1. Overview

BeamDocs is built for local document handling on Android. Scans, OCR text, thumbnails, folders, and Smart Organization choices stay on your device unless you choose to open or share a PDF with another app.

2. Data Collection

OfflineNode does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, or sell your document contents, scanned images, OCR text, folders, or local organization data.

  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use activity-tracking analytics.
  • We do not upload your documents or scanned images to our servers.

3. Local Storage

Scanned documents, generated PDFs, extracted text, and related metadata are stored locally in BeamDocs app storage on your device.

This data is used only to provide app features such as:

  • document saving,
  • file organization,
  • local search,
  • OCR-based text indexing,
  • Smart Organization choices.

4. Permissions

BeamDocs may request only the permissions required for core app functionality, such as:

  • Camera access, to scan documents,
  • Storage or media access, if needed for saving, importing, or exporting files.

These permissions are used only for the intended functionality of the app.

5. No Document Cloud

BeamDocs does not require an account, does not run a document cloud, and does not upload your document files for processing or organization.

6. OCR and On-Device Processing

BeamDocs may use on-device machine learning and OCR technologies to detect document edges, improve scans, and extract text.

All such processing is performed locally on your device. Your images and extracted text are not sent to OfflineNode servers.

7. Optional Diagnostics

BeamDocs does not use analytics to track your activity. Optional diagnostic reports may be enabled manually to help us identify crashes and bugs.

Diagnostic reports do not include document contents, scanned images, or OCR text.

8. Sharing to Other Apps

If you open or share a PDF with email, cloud storage, or another app, that action happens outside BeamDocs and is always your choice.

9. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical measures to protect data stored by the app. The security of your data also depends on the security of your device, including your screen lock, PIN, password, or biometric protection.

OfflineNode is not responsible for unauthorized access resulting from loss, theft, compromise, or improper protection of your device.

10. Data Sharing

BeamDocs does not share your personal data or documents with third parties.

11. Children's Privacy

BeamDocs is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
